# Discovery evaluation stage

The Evaluation data tab searches literature-linked ChEMBL binding measurements in an explicit 500/1,000/2,000-record window. It reports the upstream record count separately from inspected and eligible counts. Original values, units, qualifiers, documents, assay metadata, source IDs and exclusions are retained. Direct single-protein assay assignment and species are checked. Positive molar concentrations become pX = −log10(X / mol L⁻¹); Kd, Ki and IC50 remain separate. Censored values stay visible as inverted bounds and do not enter exact quantitative ranking. Conflicting replicates, duplicates and unsupported ligand chemistry are excluded explicitly.

The 500- or 1,000-record search contributes every eligible measurement compatible with the selected experimental receptor; there is no 8/24-compound subset. Assay/endpoint/variant cohorts remain separate, including repeated compounds measured in different assays. Every retained measurement is evaluated. At least one cohort needs three measurements for ranking; smaller or constant-label cohorts have no correlation metric. Known mutation mismatches and unknown declared crystal variants are blocked. Remaining construct/condition equivalence and pretrained training overlap are unverified: this stage is an exploratory comparison, not held-out predictive validation. It computes rank correlations for each protocol; it does not fit/calibrate scores, infer inactive labels, convert energy to experimental pKd, or automatically select a winner.

The reviewed fixed preprocessing retains one target protein chain, omits other chains/waters/hetero residues, and proposes omission of incomplete terminal residues only if all their deposited atoms are more than 12 Å from the reference ligand. Exact omissions are listed in the frozen run details. Internal missing atoms or incomplete residues near the ligand block preparation. No heavy atoms are repaired or invented. Meeko prepares the receptor, and RDKit ETKDGv3 uses seed 42 while retaining supplied ligand charge and tautomer. Preparation menu entries remain UI-only. Pocket methods are a runnable stage: fpocket 4.2.3 predicts from protein coordinates alone, and Crystal ligand pocket is an explicit known-site baseline. fpocket rank (default 1), padding (4 Å) and minimum box size (20 Å) are frozen; boxes over 50 Å fail visibly. P2Rank/DeepPocket have no connected adapters and are excluded with reasons. No predicted pocket is chosen using proximity to the reference ligand. Pocket geometry and provenance are recorded under each run’s pockets directory.

**Start** below the protein field now automatically selects a usable crystal reference and the full compatible eligible evaluation set from the chosen search, freezes the protocol, and starts a separate credential-filtered worker. The frozen plan records dataset, source coordinates, CCD chemistry, parameters, executable/adapter fingerprints, exact reference changes and exclusions. Vina/GNINA/Uni-Dock generate poses, with Vina, GNINA CNN pose and GNINA affinity rescoring where executable. Unavailable/unsupported candidates remain exclusions. GNINA docking defaults to one CPU; affinity rescoring respects the selected device. Each native call has a 120-second timeout. New full-set runs have no 30-minute overall cutoff: they continue in the background until finished or explicitly stopped. Stop requests cancel native process groups. Identical docking requests share poses across scorers and repeated assay measurements, but pocket method/parameters and box are included in the cache identity; outputs remain immutable per run.

The Evaluation run tab refreshes active stage/combination and completed rows every two seconds. Spearman and Kendall statistics require every requested measured compound to finish; partial failure is not hidden by dropping rows. Native scores retain their own units. PoseBusters results and runtime are separate columns. RMSD and crystal-coordinate rescoring comparisons appear only for the same chemical identity. Other ligands can be overlaid with the crystal binder for binding-site inspection but are never assigned a redocking RMSD. The viewer verifies pose hashes; orange is docked ligand and cyan is the crystal ligand.

Runs live in `.biocentric/evaluation-runs/<id>` and can be reopened using `?evaluation=<id>`. No benchmark starts from reload or target entry alone. One explicit Start click prepares and launches the evaluation; there is no separate candidate-pool acceptance. Advanced Evaluation data choices remain optional, and startup failures are visible and retryable. Changing tools or review inputs requires another Start action. Repeated use of public evaluation results for tuning requires a fresh independent holdout before making predictive-performance claims.

Verification: `tests/test_discovery_benchmark.py` uses explicitly synthetic labels to check normalization, censoring, species, duplicate/conflict exclusions, mutation matching, unsafe reference trimming, complete-pair accounting, approval invalidation, cancellation, integrity and worker result flow. `runs/evaluation-engine-smoke/verified-rescoring.json` records actual Vina/GNINA/Uni-Dock fixture docking and rescoring checks on the mouse 1IEP software example. Those results are not human ABL1 activity validation.

The Pipeline map tab flows horizontally through Protein → Pocket → Docking → Scoring → Ligands. Protein summarizes fixed preparation. Pocket, docking and scoring choices each form their own column, with all adjacent-stage connections. Historical runs retain their actual crystal-defined site instead of falsely showing a predicted method. A compact list shows up to seven evaluation ligands and the remaining count, keeping the active ligand visible. Running paths have animated cyan links and the current stage is highlighted. Gray means untested, yellow limited/mixed evidence, and green requires complete finite results, all physical checks, identity-matched crystal RMSD ≤2 Å, at least five measurements, and a positive lower bound of a deterministic 95% stratified-bootstrap interval for the equal-weight mean within-assay Spearman correlation (2,000 resamples). A shared upstream link is green only if every evaluated path it covers is green. These are evidence-strength colors on this stratified evaluation set, not calibrated confidence probabilities or causal tool-quality estimates. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation.

Final results ranks eligible completed chains by oriented Spearman, then Kendall, with deterministic ID ties. All other protocols and exclusions remain visible. Runtime is reported but not used to rank because poses are reused. The user explicitly chooses **Lock final chain**; there is no automatic final selection. The action writes an immutable `decision.json` with exact tool parameters, fixed preprocessing, reference, policy, statistics, and frozen-plan/result hashes, after verifying pose files. Failed/partial compound accounting, substituted labels, unmatched reference controls and invalid poses prevent locking. A new evaluation is required to change a locked decision. This is an exploratory post-evaluation decision policy, not a prespecified independent validation.

Final visualizations shows the locked chain's crystal overlay, native and experimental scores, the same compound's results across other methods, and an experimental-vs-oriented-model-score plot. Only chemically matched crystal controls receive pose-recovery comparisons. Tests in `tests/test_pipeline_decisions.py` cover color/ranking gates, identity and accounting failures, immutable decisions, altered artifacts, graph connections, HTML escaping, and final-view UI interaction using explicitly synthetic fixtures.

The root page is the experiment library. Each row opens a durable workspace; Create new experiment starts with a blank target, empty chat and default tools. The table refreshes background statuses every three seconds and reports evaluated tool combinations out of total runnable combinations, independent of the number of measurements, plus the saved final chain (or an eligible candidate awaiting a decision). A stopped or failed evaluation is never shown as completed.

Workspaces are saved under `.biocentric/experiments/<id>/workspace.json`. Snapshots retain chat, target identity, source evidence, literature, the searched evaluation dataset, custom plugins, tool parameters and inspection choices. Worker files and final decisions remain in their existing immutable run directories. Navigation changes only the displayed workspace; workers continue independently, so multiple experiments may run while the library or another target is open. Rerunning an evaluated experiment creates a separate library entry and preserves the prior run. Opening a locked experiment defaults to Final visualizations, using the original `decision.json` and verified pose files. Experiments without a final choice remain explicitly undecided.

Existing runs are automatically indexed. Older runs that did not save a full workspace restore their frozen evaluation subset and recover public structure/literature panels through the existing source cache. The missing full historical search is disclosed in Evaluation data; no evidence or final decision is fabricated. `?experiment=<id>` opens a saved workspace; older `?evaluation=<id>` links remain supported.

New plans use schema 2. A combination is pocket method × docking tool × scoring method; one combination finishes only after all retained measurements and the crystal control have been attempted, including pose validation. Failed attempts are labeled Failed; unrun/cancelled rows do not advance combination progress. Measurement count is displayed separately. Old saved plans retain their original test sets and methods; their displayed progress is derived in combination units without rewriting the original artifacts.

Spearman and Kendall metrics are calculated separately per assay/endpoint/variant cohort and then averaged equally over cohorts with at least three nonconstant experimental labels. A constant prediction in an otherwise rankable cohort makes the aggregate unavailable; it cannot improve a chain by dropping a difficult assay. All frozen measurements must succeed before correlations are reported. The 2,000-resample stratified bootstrap resamples paired measurements independently within each cohort and averages the replicate correlations. Invalid replicates and small groups may prevent an interval; no confidence is invented. Per-assay metrics are available beside the aggregate; final score plots select a single assay. This does not establish independent predictive validation.

The Bio Orchestrator workspace uses a full-height 1:2 layout: phase/tool selectors are stacked in the left control column, followed by a single-line protein target and Start/Stop. The tool list scrolls independently so target entry stays visible. The right column contains the experiment tabs and visualizations from top to bottom. Tool parameters remain behind each phase's settings button.

Each library row has a delete action with confirmation. Deletion requests cancellation of that experiment's running worker and marks its workspace (and recovery aliases) deleted. The library and legacy-run importer do not resurrect deleted entries, and stale browser sessions cannot save or start a deleted workspace. Local artifacts are retained for recovery. Undo delete restores the row but never starts a worker or clears a prior cancellation request.
